NLP · Dashboard 87.6% · 0.88 F1

SentIQ — AI Market Research Dashboard

Problem. Thousands of chaotic customer reviews are worthless until a business can act on them.

Approach. A model showdown (SVM vs BERT vs RoBERTa) + aspect-based sentiment with DeBERTa-v3, and a Groq-powered LLaMA 3.3 70B assistant that writes grounded executive summaries. Built in Streamlit.

Result. RoBERTa won at 87.6% accuracy; ships one-click AI reports, aspect breakdowns, and side-by-side model charts.

Time-Series · Research 93.8% · LSTM

Predictive Policing in India

Problem. Policing is reactive — hotspot maps look backward. Can we forecast crime so resources move before incidents pile up?

Approach. Benchmarked Prophet, NeuralProphet & LSTM across 6,336 records — 705 districts, 25 crime categories (2014–22) — then built a Crime Data Visualizer web app.

Result. LSTM won at 93.8% accuracy, generating 2023–25 district forecasts + a hotspot ranking system. Co-authored research paper (draft).

GitHub Paper · draft in progress
Behavioural Analytics 213 respondents · 6 patterns

Dark Patterns in Food-Delivery Apps

Problem. Do manipulative UX tricks on Zomato & Swiggy — fake timers, hidden fees, pre-added items — actually make people overspend, and who's most vulnerable?

Approach. Surveyed 213 people against 6 documented dark patterns, then ran real statistics: correlation, chi-square, ANOVA, logistic regression & K-Means clustering.

Result. Segmented users into three behavioural clusters — "Easy Target," "Aware but Affected," and "Immune" — with manipulation exposure tracking unplanned spend.

GitHub Live dashboard
Multi-Agent LLMs · Team research In progress

Counterfactual Debiasing in Multi-Agent LLM Systems

Problem. Teams of AI agents are being proposed to make high-stakes calls like loan approvals — but research shows their debate can amplify demographic bias by up to 10×, and no fix exists that's built for the multi-agent setting.

Approach. Building and benchmarking debate-time fixes — most centrally a counterfactual "Devil's Advocate" agent that re-argues each case with gender/age flipped and flags bias before the verdict — against single-agent and unmitigated baselines on the German Credit & Adult Income datasets.

Where it's at. Implementing the baseline debate pipeline and three mitigation conditions now, measuring approval-rate disparity and counterfactual flip-rate. Aiming for a Scopus-indexed IEEE/Springer conference.
